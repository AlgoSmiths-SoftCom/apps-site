Taste Match
by AlgoSmiths

How alike are you, really?

Pick 10 favourites across artists, songs, movies, shows, food and places, or a theme like Music or Khana & places. Send one link to the group. Two ways to play: friends pick their own favourites and see how alike you are, or they guess yours and see how well they know you. Every answer gets a score, the reasons behind it, and a card made for Status and Stories. You get a leaderboard of who knows you best, revealed when you are ready.

Coming soon on Google Play

Android first. On an iPhone? An iOS version comes later if enough people ask.

Friends only, private by design

  • No account, no phone number, no contacts, no location. Just a display name you choose.
  • No profiles, no chat, no matching with strangers. Only people with your link can take your quiz.
  • Your full list stays private. Friends see only the picks you both chose.
  • Quizzes expire after 90 days. Delete everything any time from Settings.
  • No ads, no subscription, nothing locked, no analytics, nothing sold. Picks and results are stored on our server (Supabase, Mumbai) so your friends can take your quiz.

18 and over

Taste Match is for adults. Names and free-text picks can be reported from inside the app, and hosts can remove anyone from their own leaderboard.
