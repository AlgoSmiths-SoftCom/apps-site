Taste Match privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026. Applies to the Taste Match Android app (package com.algosmiths.tastematch) by AlgoSmiths SoftCom LLP, India.

The short version

  • We store a display name you choose, the favourites you pick, your results, the people you block, and the time you accepted the terms. Nothing else.
  • No account, no email, no phone number, no contacts, no location, no photos, no device identifiers, no ads, no analytics, no sale of data.
  • Your data is stored on our server so friends can take your quiz. Quizzes expire after 90 days. You can delete everything at once from Settings.

Standard server logs (IP address, time, request) are kept by our hosting provider, Supabase, for security, for the period set by their platform policy. We do not use them to profile anyone.

Where it is stored

On Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL) in the Mumbai, India region, over encrypted connections. Supabase acts as our processor. There is no other third party: no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising network.

How long

  • Quizzes, picks and results: deleted automatically 90 days after the quiz was created.
  • Your display name: deleted when you delete your data, or automatically 90 days after your last activity if you have no quizzes or results left.
  • Everything at once: Settings → Delete my data. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. Friends who took your quiz lose their result for it.

Sharing and links

A quiz link contains only a random 6-character code. Anyone with the link can see your display name and the category mix of your quiz, and can take it. Share cards you create contain your names, the percentage and the shared picks; where you send them is up to you.

Age

Taste Match is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the app, email us and we will delete the data.

Your rights (including under India's DPDP Act)

You can see your data in the app, correct your display name in the app, and erase everything with Delete my data. For anything else, or to withdraw consent, email contact@algosmiths.com. We aim to answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will show the notice again before you continue.
