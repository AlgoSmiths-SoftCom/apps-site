How rough is your road?

Khadda Meter by AlgoSmiths

Start a trip, put your phone in your pocket or a mount, and ride or drive as usual. When you stop, Khadda Meter gives you a road score from 0 (butter smooth) to 10 (khadda zone), bumps per km, and the three worst stretches on your route.

Coming soon on Google Play

Android first. On an iPhone? An iOS version comes later if enough people ask.

Private by design

  • Trips are processed and stored on your phone and never uploaded. Maps use OpenStreetMap tiles for the area on screen.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics. Your data is never sold.
  • Location is used only while a trip you started is running.
  • Share cards hide the first and last 200 m of your route.

Safety first

Do not handle the phone while riding or driving. Khadda Meter scores the road surface only. It does not judge your driving and is not a navigation or safety device.
