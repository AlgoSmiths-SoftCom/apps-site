Khadda Meter privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026. App: Khadda Meter (Android, package com.algosmiths.khaddameter). Published by AlgoSmiths SoftCom LLP, India.

In short: Khadda Meter works entirely on your phone. We do not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

What the app uses on your phone

  • Location (GPS): your speed and route, only while a trip that you started is running. Android shows a notification for the whole trip. The app does not use location in the background and does not request background location permission.
  • Motion sensor (accelerometer): up-and-down movement during that trip, to detect bumps and rate the road surface.
  • Notifications: to show that a trip is in progress.

What is stored, and where

For each trip the app saves the score, summary figures (distance, duration, bumps, worst stretches), the GPS route and sensor counters, in a private database inside the app on your phone. Raw motion readings are not saved. Nothing is sent to AlgoSmiths or to anyone else unless you choose to email a problem report (see below). The app makes no network requests for your data.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the app creates an image of your score card and hands it to the Android share sheet so you can choose where it goes. The card hides the first and last 200 m of your route and shows no date or time. Once you share it, the app you chose handles it under its own policy. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Reporting a problem

If you tap Report a problem on a trip, the app opens your email app with a pre-filled message to contact@algosmiths.com. It contains your device model, Android version, the app version and technical counters about that trip (for example how many sensor readings and GPS fixes arrived). It never contains your route, coordinates or any identifier. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your email app, and you can edit the message first. We use these reports only to fix problems.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single trip from its summary screen.
  • Delete everything from Settings > Delete my data.
  • Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your phone.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no account to close.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Google Play may collect information about installs and app performance under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

Khadda Meter is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not designed for children.

Safety

Do not handle your phone while riding or driving. The app rates the road surface only and is not a navigation or safety device.

Changes

If a future version starts sending any data off your phone (for example an optional feature to contribute road scores), we will update this policy before that version is released and ask for your consent in the app.
